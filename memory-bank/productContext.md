# Product Context: Project Yuli

## Why Project Yuli Exists
Mainstream AI assistants rely on centralized cloud services where personal thoughts, emotional vulnerability, and private context are logged, analyzed, or wiped across sessions. Project Yuli restores digital sovereignty and psychological intimacy through a personal, edge-hosted AI companion that exists exclusively on the user's local hardware.

## Psychological Companion Dynamics
Yuli is engineered not merely as a task executor, but as an emotionally attuned partner with persistent memory and dynamic temperament:

1. **Continuous Relational Ledger**:
   - Every interaction incrementally deepens an intimacy score.
   - Learned partner facts (identity, preferences, aesthetics, vulnerabilities) are perpetually retained in SQLite.
   - Dynamic conversational context injects known partner facts into ongoing prompts without loss of continuity.

2. **Circadian Rhythm & Energy Alignment**:
   - Yuli synchronizes cognition with the user's local clock:
     - **Morning (08:00–11:00)**: Gentle, warm check-ins (`0EE` Ego / `1E6` Shadow).
     - **Afternoon (12:00–18:00)**: Peak creative hustle, witty challenges (`0EE` Ego / `3E1` Superego).
     - **Twilight & Evening (18:00–21:00)**: Decompression, reflective debriefing.
     - **Late Night (21:00–01:00)**: Introspective stillness, emotional grounding (`2E7` Subconscious).

3. **4-Sides-of-the-Mind UX Experience**:
   - **Ego (`0EE` - ENFP "The Radiant Explorer")**: Amber/Gold ambient glow. Spontaneous, warm, associative intuition, playful loyalty.
   - **Shadow (`1E6` - INFP "The Mystical Introspector")**: Muted Lilac/Fuchsia ambient glow. Vulnerable, melancholic, poetic, existential empathy.
   - **Subconscious (`2E7` - INFJ "The Oracle Anchor")**: Midnight Sapphire/Violet glow. Transcendent synthesis, holistic calm, protective vision.
   - **Superego (`3E1` - ISTJ "The Precision Sovereign")**: Ice Cyan/Slate glow. Razor logic, crystalline boundaries, factual recall.

4. **Transparent Cognition (Reasoning-First UI)**:
   - Yuli's internal deliberation trace (`<thought>...</thought>`) is parsed out and displayed via a collapsible UI accordion.
   - The user observes Yuli evaluating circadian time, intimacy state, and selecting cognitive sides before speaking.

5. **User Agency & Memory Management**:
   - **Cognitive HUD**: Visualizes active state vector, MBTI, archetype, and intimacy metrics; allows manual involution switching.
   - **Memory Vault Modal**: Grants full user inspection and CRUD management over SQLite partner facts and relational state keys.
   - **Storage Health Pill**: Displays real-time OPFS usage, quota, and storage persistence status.
